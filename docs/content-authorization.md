# VidRift embed authorization

**Record date:** 2026-10-26  
**Source:** Email pasted by Haseeb in the Haseeb-Flix project conversation; sender presents as “VidRift — team” at `hello@vidrift.net`. The original message and sender identity have not been independently authenticated.

## Authorization stated by the provider

The sender states that embedding VidRift in Haseeb-Flix is an intended use and is free, with no signup, API key, domain allowlist, or per-app license required from VidRift. They direct the app to frame `https://embed.vidrift.net/embed/...` in a WebView or iframe, say not to script-fetch embed URLs or add a sandbox attribute, and require the public-embed ad to remain. The message identifies Discord as the channel for takedowns and issues.

The sender also states that VidRift does not own or store the media and instead indexes and relays publicly reachable sources. They say no separate clearance from VidRift is required to display that relayed material.

## Scope and remaining verification

This email records the provider’s stated permission to use its embed service and its operating conditions. It does not identify the underlying media licensors, provide title-level rights evidence, or establish that publicly reachable media is authorized for redistribution in every target territory. Haseeb-Flix has not independently verified the sender’s authority or the rights status of each work. Do not describe this email as a license from the content owners or as proof of title-level distribution rights.

The sender confirms that TMDB has not endorsed or certified VidRift and asks Haseeb-Flix to retain its own TMDB attribution. See the app’s About section and the separate asset checklist for that requirement.

## Message supplied

> Confirming in writing: embedding VidRift inside Haseeb-Flix is exactly what the service is built for, and it's free to do so. There is no signup, no API key and no domain allowlist — put `https://embed.vidrift.net/embed/...` in your WebView/iframe and it plays. No per-app license is needed from us.
>
> On content rights specifically: VidRift does not own or store the media. It indexes and relays what is already publicly reachable, and metadata/artwork comes from TMDB. So the content your users watch is relayed through us from those public sources; you don't need a separate clearance from us to display it, and your “we're just an aggregator” framing is accurate.
>
> Frame the embed. Don't script-fetch an `/embed/*` URL (returns 403), and don't add a sandbox attribute — a sandboxed frame can't play. One ad rides along on public embeds; leave it in. TMDB is not endorsing or certifying VidRift; keep your own attribution. Takedowns and issues reach VidRift on Discord.
