About HaseebFlix

HaseebFlix helps you decide what to watch next. See what is trending, top rated and coming soon, search for any movie or TV show, and read the story, cast and crew before you pick. Found something good? Tap Play and watch it right inside the app, or tap the heart to save it for later.

What’s new

Watch inside the app. Every movie and TV show now has a Play button. For TV shows, choose the season and episode first.

What you can do

  • Always something newHome shows trending, top rated, most popular, now playing and upcoming titles, for both movies and TV shows.
  • Find a title fastType a name in Search and switch between movies and TV shows.
  • Know before you watchSee the year, length, genres, rating, story, cast and crew on every title.
  • Watch in one tapPress Play to start. Controls such as fullscreen and subtitles are built into the player when a video offers them.
  • Keep a wishlistTap the heart on anything you want to watch later and find it again in Wishlist.
  • Quick to reopenPages you have already opened load faster and stay available when your connection is weak.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android and wait for it to finish.
  2. Open the file. Tap the notification, or find it in your Files or Downloads app.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps: tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If a Play Protect message appears, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  5. Tap Open and start browsing.

Questions

Why does Android show a warning?

HaseebFlix is not on Google Play, so Android asks you to confirm the install. This is normal for apps downloaded from a website. Only install apps from sources you trust.

Does it cost anything?

No. HaseebFlix is free to download and use.

Where do the videos come from?

Videos are played by an outside video provider inside the app, so what is available and how well it plays can vary. Movie and TV information comes from TMDB.

Which phones does it work on?

Phones and tablets running Android 6.0 or newer.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Read the privacy policy for details.
