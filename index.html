Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

HaseebFlix is published and operated by Abdul Haseeb ('we', 'us'). We don't host the content on our servers — we're just an aggregator. We point you to movies and shows hosted and streamed by third-party providers.

Information in the app

HaseebFlix does not require an app account. It stores your watchlist and catalogue cache in a local database on your device. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer include that database; other app files and preferences are excluded by the app's backup rules. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes the local database. Device backup copies may remain according to your Android backup settings.

TMDB catalogue and images

When you browse or search, HaseebFlix requests catalogue information from The Movie Database (TMDB) and artwork from its image service. These services receive request details and ordinary network information, such as your IP address. The TMDB API key is included in the app and can be extracted from an APK; it is not a private server credential and is not saved in the app database.

Playback and advertising

When you start playback, HaseebFlix loads a VidRift player inside an embedded WebView. The request includes the selected title identifiers, and VidRift may receive your IP address and ordinary connection details. VidRift has told us that its embedded player uses its own storage and that public embeds show one ad. The exact storage items, ad recipients, tracking, retention and onward sharing have not been independently verified. The player may connect to services operated by VidRift or its advertising partners.

We do not control the data practices of TMDB, VidRift, or services reached inside the player. You can avoid playback-related requests by not starting a video. Clearing HaseebFlix data does not delete information held by those third parties.

Information sharing and retention

HaseebFlix sends the requests needed to retrieve catalogue information and start playback. We do not operate an app account system. Your watchlist and catalogue cache remain in the local app database until you clear app data, uninstall the app, or remove a backup copy. Retention by TMDB, VidRift and any player advertising partners is controlled by those services and has not been independently confirmed.

Your choices

You can clear HaseebFlix's app data or uninstall the app to remove its local database. You can choose not to start playback. Android backup and restore are controlled by your device settings.
