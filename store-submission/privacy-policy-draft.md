HaseebFlix is an aggregator and does not host content on its servers. It points to media streamed through third-party providers.

## Information handled by HaseebFlix

HaseebFlix does not require an app account. The app stores your watchlist and catalogue cache in a local Room database. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer include that database. Other app files and preferences are excluded by the app's backup rules. The TMDB API key is included in the installed app and is not stored in that database.

When you browse or search, the app requests catalogue information and artwork from The Movie Database (TMDB) and its image service. Those requests include the information needed for the request and your network address, which the services may process under their own policies.

## Playback and third-party services

When you start playback, HaseebFlix loads a VidRift player inside an embedded WebView. The player receives the requested title identifiers and may receive your IP address and ordinary connection details. VidRift has told the publisher that its embedded player uses its own storage and that public embeds show one ad. The exact storage, ad recipients, cookies, tracking, retention and onward sharing have not yet been independently verified. The player and its ad may connect to services outside HaseebFlix.

HaseebFlix does not control the privacy practices of TMDB, VidRift, or other services reached by the player. Please review their policies before use once the publisher confirms the relevant links.

## Choices and retention

Your watchlist and catalogue cache remain in the app database until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Device backups may retain a copy under the backup settings on your device. You can avoid sending playback requests by not starting a video. Clearing HaseebFlix app data removes its local database; it does not delete information retained by third-party services.
